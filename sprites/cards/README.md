# Playing cards

Placeholder faces until the Lea has its own deck. From Byron Knoll's
"Vector Playing Cards" 1.3 (CC0 / public domain),
https://opengameart.org/content/playing-cards-vector-png , scaled to 160×232.
Named `<rank><suit>.png`: rank 2–10 J Q K A, suit S H D C. The back is drawn in CSS
(`js/cards-view.js`). Court cards are the `…2.png` variants (full art).
